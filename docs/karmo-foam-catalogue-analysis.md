# Karmo Foam Catalogue Analysis

Source: `karmo-foam-catalogue.pdf` (27 A4 pages, Karmo Group, 2026 catalogue edition)

## Brand story and positioning

- Karmo presents itself as Bangladesh's first producer of polyurethane products, with a manufacturing journey beginning in 1965.
- The core promise is practical comfort backed by material engineering: density, resilience, durability, fine-cell structure, airflow and custom sizing.
- Repeated proof points are "No Filler Used", rubber-grade foam, local manufacturing, ISO 9001, UKAS and Moody International certification.
- The catalogue connects foam to real applications rather than treating it as an abstract material: furniture, beds, automotive interiors, rickshaws, footwear, acoustic treatment, packaging and garments.
- Brand colors are Karmo red, black, white and a strong catalogue yellow. Product-grade colors act as secondary identifiers.

All main grades are presented as no-filler products and as suitable for furniture, bedding and automotive interior applications. Standard bed cuts run from 0.5 to 4 inches in common 72-inch lengths, with custom cutting available.

## Beds and automotive findings used in the banners

### Beds

- Catalogue cues: hospital-bed safety, elasticity, strength, foldable options, custom sizes and visco-elastic pressure response.
- Meaningful image language: visible mattress layers, a soft pressure contour, fine-cell texture and a stable high-density support base.
- Copy territory: pressure relief, responsive layers, restorative support and custom-cut sizing.

### Automotive

- Catalogue cues: seats, roof liners, dashboards, instrument panels, rickshaw and auto-rickshaw cushioning.
- Meaningful image language: a shaped seat-core cutaway, multiple foam densities, upholstery recovery and cabin integration.
- Copy territory: seat cushioning, fine-cell resilience, load-bearing support and cut-to-fit profiles.

### Combined story

- The strongest shared idea is one material science serving two environments: sleep and travel.
- A continuous foam layer visually connecting mattress and seat makes the relationship understandable without technical diagrams.
- Karmo red is used as a restrained seam/accent while the interface carries the official Karmo mark and exact typography.

## Banner implementation decisions

- Three 3840 x 2160 WebP assets: combined, beds and automotive.
- The artwork contains no generated text or logo. Official Karmo artwork and accessible HTML copy sit above the photography.
- Desktop composition reserves a dark left field for copy. Mobile crops retain the material cutaway and move the content toward the lower third.
