38 designed screens — 18 storefront and 20 admin panel — all responsive, all built on one design system covering colour, typography, spacing and motion.

## 🛠️ Core Technical Features & Scope

### 1. 🛍️ Customer Storefront & Pages

- 📱 **Fully Responsive Design:** Flawless experience across mobile, tablet and desktop.
- 🎨 **Premium Homepage:** Dynamic hero, featured divisions, best sellers, new arrivals, gallery and video sections.
- 🏭 **Four Division Sections:** Foam, Mattress, HomeTex and Chemicals — each with its own landing page and sub-pages (13 in total).
- 🔍 **Advanced Product Listing:** Filters by division, category, price, size, density and stock status, with smart sorting.
- 📦 **Detailed Product Page:** 10-image gallery, motion video, variants (size / thickness / density / colour), full specification table, and related products.
- 🎙️ **Smart Search:** Instant live search across product name, code and description.
- 📄 **Complete Page Set:** Home, Shop, About Us, Company History, Board of Directors, Awards, Partners, Store Locator, Blog, FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Refund/Return Policy, Contact, Bulk Order Enquiry.

### 2. 🏭 Wholesale & Dealer Portal

- 🔐 **Approval-Gated Registration:** Dealers register with business name, trade licence and address — the account activates only after your team approves it.
- 💰 **Separate Wholesale Pricing:** Wholesale rates are completely invisible to the public and to unapproved accounts.
- 📊 **Quantity-Based Price Tiers:** For example 10–49 units at one rate, 50+ at another — configurable per product.
- 📦 **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** Set individually per product and enforced at cart level.
- 📋 **Bulk Order Enquiry:** A dedicated route for project and institutional buyers.

### 3. 🔐 Customer Accounts & Security

- 🔑 **Secure Authentication:** Registration, login and robust password reset.
- 🛡️ **Verification:** Email verification and OTP for genuine user onboarding.
- 📍 **Profile Management:** Customer dashboard with multiple saved shipping addresses and full order history.
- 💖 **Wishlist:** Save and share favourite products.
- 👤 **Guest Checkout:** Instant retail checkout with an account generated automatically.

### 4. 🛒 Cart & Checkout System

- 🛒 **Smart Shopping Cart:** Quantity control, "Save for Later", and a cart that follows the customer across devices.
- 🎟️ **Promo Engine:** Coupon and discount code validation, by product or category, with scheduled start and end dates.
- 🚚 **Automated Delivery Charges:** Location-based shipping calculation, with free delivery above a configurable order value.
- ⚡ **Optimised Checkout:** Clean, conversion-focused single-page flow.
- 🔄 **Live Stock Validation:** Stock re-checked at the moment of payment, with a clear message if an item has just sold out.

### 5. 💳 Payment Gateway Integration

- 📱 **bKash Integration:** Direct and secure automated bKash API.
- 💳 **SSLCommerz:** All major debit and credit cards, internet banking and mobile wallets.
- 💵 **Cash on Delivery (COD):** With the option to restrict by area or order value.
- 🔄 **Transaction Logs:** Secure logging with one-click retry for failed payments.

### 6. 📦 Order Management & Automated Delivery

- 🔄 **Full Order Lifecycle:** Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Ready to Ship → Out for Delivery → Delivered, plus Cancelled, Returned and Refunded states.
- 👥 **Role-Separated Workflow:** Each stage is moved by a specific staff role — the Order Confirmer confirms, the Processor packs, the Delivery Agent delivers. No one can act outside their stage.
- 📍 **Public Order Tracking:** Real-time tracking by Order ID, no login required.
- 🚀 **Courier API Integration:** One-click booking (Pathao & Steadfast) with automatic tracking number generation and live status sync.
- 🛑 **Self-Cancellation:** Customers can cancel from their dashboard before dispatch.
- 📉 **Automatic Stock Control:** Stock is held on order, deducted on confirmation, and released automatically on cancellation.

### 7. 📄 Automated Invoices & Documentation

- 📑 **PDF Invoice Generation:** Professional PDF invoice created automatically for every order.
- ✉️ **Email Automation:** Invoice emailed instantly on successful order placement.
- 🖨️ **Print Ready:** Customers and admins can download or print invoices and packing slips at any time.

### 8. ↩️ Returns & Refund System

- 📸 **Smart Return Requests:** Customers submit a reason with proof photographs.
- 🎛️ **Admin Moderation:** Full control to approve or reject requests and process refunds.
- 🕒 **Status Tracking:** Dedicated status updates for every return.

### 9. 📊 Admin Panel & Multi-Role Staff Control

- 📈 **Business Insights:** Dashboard with sales analytics, revenue charts, pending confirmations and low-stock alerts.
- 📥 **Inventory Control:** Add and edit products, manage variants, bulk CSV upload, low-stock alerts.
- 👥 **Customer Management:** Retail and wholesale lists, wholesale approval queue, review moderation, coupons.
- 📝 **Complete Activity Log:** Every action by every staff member recorded with user and timestamp — fully auditable.

**🔐 Six Staff Roles with Granular Permissions:**

1. **Super Admin** — Full control: staff, permissions and system settings.
2. **Admin** — All daily operations: products, prices, orders, customers, reports.
3. **Manager** — Full visibility and intervention, without running daily stages.
4. **Order Confirmer** — Verifies and confirms or cancels incoming orders.
5. **Order Processor** — Picks, packs and prepares confirmed orders.
6. **Delivery Agent** — Records dispatch, delivery and COD collection.

A single employee can hold multiple roles where your operation requires it.

### 10. 🚀 Marketing, SEO & Infrastructure

- 🔍 **SEO & Speed:** Next.js architecture for fast loads. Meta tags, Open Graph, automatic sitemap.xml and robots.txt, and structured product data for Google Shopping eligibility.
- 📊 **Analytics Integration:** Google Analytics for visitor tracking and conversion measurement.
- 💾 **Database & Backend:** Node.js and MongoDB with encrypted passwords and token-based authentication (JWT).
- 🌐 **Deployment Ready:** Cloud image hosting with CDN, custom domain mapping and full SSL security.
- 🗄️ **Automated Daily Backups:** Database backed up daily, automatically.
